APUSH HEXAGON STUDY TOOL

This tool is meant to generate conversations between yourself and your study partners. As you connect Hexagons and make connections, discuss with your study partners WHY you chose these connections. The more connections you can make, and the more conversations you can generate, the better prepared you will be to understand cause and effect, context, comparisons, and the other skills that AP tests require.

How it works

  1. DragPull terms from the tray onto the table.
  2. FlipDouble-click a hexagon to see its date and definition. Click a flipped hexagon to read it on a large card.
  3. ColorClick a hexagon to color-code it.
  4. ConnectPush two sides together, then pick how they connect, in what area, and (if you like) why.
  5. TimelineSwitch to Timeline to see your terms laid out by date, with your connections drawn as arcs.
  6. Mental Word BankPick one of the study guide's questions and surround it with the terms that help answer it.
